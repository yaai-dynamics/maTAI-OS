# 01 — Product Vision

## Product name

**maTAI**

Manipur Tourism Intelligence Platform. Ma(nipur) + T(ourism) + AI — and
"matai" is also a word for the edge, border or flanking side.

Working interface names:
- **Explore Manipur** — tourist experience
- **Decision Room** — government intelligence
- **Create for Manipur** — creator hub

## One-line positioning

> An intelligent tourism ecosystem connecting tourists, local tourism businesses, creators and the Tourism Department through a shared Manipur tourism knowledge and intelligence layer.

## Core problem

Manipur has rich destinations, heritage, culture, nature, local businesses and events. The tourism experience and tourism-management workflow are fragmented:

- Tourists discover disconnected information and often lack contextual, personalized experiences.
- Tourism businesses are difficult for tourists to discover in a structured way.
- Creators can promote destinations, but campaigns are often fragmented from government objectives and measurable tourism outcomes.
- Tourism departments need a consolidated way to understand visitor signals, destination demand, issues, local capacity and campaign performance.

## Product thesis

The platform should create a closed loop:

> **Promote → Discover → Plan → Experience → Visit → Feedback → Learn → Improve → Promote again**

The shared data layer makes the loop useful to all roles.

## Three products

### Explore Manipur
A conversational tourist companion that:
- understands preferences in natural language;
- creates personalized journeys;
- explains heritage/culture with grounded information;
- exposes local experiences and businesses;
- adapts plans to changing conditions;
- collects voluntary feedback/check-in signals.

### Decision Room
A government decision-support interface that:
- displays a Tourism Pulse;
- compares destinations;
- detects issues and sentiment;
- manages tourism campaigns and creators;
- answers natural-language policy/operational questions;
- supports controlled what-if simulations.

### Create for Manipur
A creator ecosystem where creators can:
- discover tourism campaigns;
- apply for campaigns;
- receive AI-generated briefs grounded in verified tourism information;
- submit content;
- monitor campaign outcomes;
- earn campaign rewards.

## Shared intelligence layer

The platform maintains a structured tourism graph covering:

- destinations
- attractions
- heritage
- culture
- nature
- events
- tourism businesses
- homestays
- guides
- local experiences
- creators
- campaigns
- tourist interactions
- feedback
- capacity signals
- alerts

## Differentiation

The product is not:
- a generic AI itinerary generator;
- a generic tourism directory;
- a generic influencer management system;
- a dashboard full of unsupported predictions.

The differentiator is the **closed-loop tourism intelligence ecosystem**:

1. Government defines an objective.
2. AI helps identify a destination/campaign opportunity.
3. Creators receive structured campaigns and verified facts.
4. Tourists discover and plan through the tourist experience.
5. Tourist interactions and feedback create aggregated tourism signals.
6. Government sees campaign and destination outcomes.
7. The cycle informs the next action.

## Data truth model

Every important metric should be traceable to a provenance category:

- Official
- Partner-reported
- Platform-observed
- Public external
- Demo synthetic
- Estimated
- Forecast

Never present a synthetic metric as an official statistic.

## Long-term vision

Create the digital intelligence layer for Manipur tourism and eventually extend the model to other Northeast destinations.

Potential future expansion:
- tourism business operations;
- destination capacity planning;
- heritage management;
- sustainable tourism optimization;
- government policy simulation;
- regional creator economy;
- interstate destination networks.

## Hackathon success criteria

The judges should leave understanding:

1. This is a connected tourism ecosystem.
2. AI is doing meaningful work rather than merely chatting.
3. Government gets measurable decision support.
4. Creators get a professional path to participate and earn.
5. Tourists receive a more contextual and personalized experience.
6. The same data loop benefits all three sides.
