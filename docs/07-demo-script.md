# 07 — 5-Minute Hackathon Demo Script

## Objective

Tell one closed-loop story:

> Government identifies an opportunity → creates campaign → creator accepts → AI helps creator → tourist discovers destination → tourist experiences destination → tourist feedback becomes signal → government evaluates impact.

## Exact live order

G1 → G5 → G4 → C2 → C3 → E1 → E2 → E3 → E4 → E6 → G3 → G5 → Final ecosystem screen

## 0:00–0:30 — Opening

**Screen:** G1

Say:

> “Imagine a tourist arriving in Manipur for the first time. They have thousands of possible places, stories, experiences and local businesses—but no intelligent way to connect them.
>
> At the same time, the Tourism Department has information about destinations, tourism activity, businesses and events—but these signals are fragmented.
>
> And local creators who can promote Manipur are not connected systematically to tourism campaigns.
>
> So we asked: what if one intelligent platform could connect all three—the tourist, the creator and the Tourism Department?
>
> This is our solution: maTAI — the Manipur Tourism Intelligence Platform.”

## 0:30–1:10 — Government identifies an opportunity

**Screen:** G5

Ask:
> “Which destination should we promote to diversify tourism away from the most concentrated destinations?”

Show evidence.

Say:
> “The important part is not just the recommendation. The system shows the evidence behind it—demand signals, capacity and visitor behaviour. It is an AI decision-support layer over tourism data.”

## 1:10–1:35 — Government creates campaign

**Screen:** G4

Create campaign:
- Discover Ukhrul
- Nature + Culture
- audience 18–35
- Instagram + YouTube
- reward pool ₹20,000

Say:
> “Instead of manually searching for influencers, the platform matches the campaign with creators based on their content category, audience and previous campaign performance.”

## 1:35–2:10 — Creator accepts

**Screen:** C2

Open campaign → Apply.

Say:
> “Now I’m a creator. I can discover tourism campaigns directly inside the platform. Each campaign tells me what the government wants to achieve, who the audience is, what content is required, the deadline and the reward.”

## 2:10–2:35 — AI creator studio

**Screen:** C3

Click Generate Content Brief.

Say:
> “The platform now helps me create the content. The AI generates a campaign concept, story structure and verified facts, so creators can produce faster while staying aligned with the Tourism Department’s information.”

## 2:35–3:05 — Tourist discovers destination

**Screen:** E1

Enter:
> “I have three days. I like nature, culture and local food, and I want to avoid crowded places.”

Add the dates, two travellers and a budget of ₹25,000, then click Plan My Trip. Three options come back in the chat, each with its stays, transport, guides and cost against the budget. Click View details on the best match.

Say:
> “That content now reaches a traveller.”

## 3:05–3:30 — Personalized journey

**Screen:** E2

Show the reason on each stop, the partner stay for each night, and the cost. Click Choose this plan, then Start this trip: the live trip is now this journey.

Say:
> “Instead of giving the tourist a generic list of attractions, the platform builds a journey around their preferences. Each recommendation also explains why it was selected.”

## 3:30–4:00 — Living Heritage

**Screens:** E3 → E4

Ask:
> “Tell me something most first-time visitors don’t know about this place.”

Say:
> “We don’t want visitors to simply arrive and take a photograph. We want them to understand the place. The AI explains the destination using our curated Manipur Tourism knowledge layer.”

## 4:00–4:20 — Feedback becomes signal

**Screen:** E6

Submit:
> “Beautiful experience, but transport information was confusing.”

Say:
> “And now something important happens. This interaction is not lost. It becomes a tourism signal.”

## 4:20–4:45 — Government measures impact

**Screen:** G3

Show Ukhrul issue.

Then G5 ask:
> “Did the Discover Ukhrul campaign improve tourism interest?”

Say:
> “The department can evaluate promotion using the entire journey from content to tourism interest and destination interaction. And the platform clearly identifies whether a number is official, observed, reported or estimated.”

## 4:45–5:00 — Close

Show final ecosystem graphic.

Say:
> “So we are not building another tourism app.
>
> We are building an intelligent tourism ecosystem for Manipur.
>
> Government can promote and manage tourism.
>
> Creators can create, reach audiences and earn.
>
> Tourists can discover, understand and experience Manipur.
>
> And every interaction continuously improves the tourism intelligence layer.
>
> Explore Manipur. Create for Manipur. Improve Manipur Tourism.
>
> Thank you.”

Show G1 → G5 → E1 → C2 → final graphic.
